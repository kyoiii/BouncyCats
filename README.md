Bouncy Cats (=^･ω･^=)

Aim:
- Eat snacks and smaller cats to grow as big as possible
- Dodge bigger cats and bounce off cats your own size

How to Play:
- Click the arrows to choose your cat.
- Press Space to start, then move your mouse to steer.
- If you get eaten, press Space to play again.

Treats:
- Bubble tea speeds you up for 7 seconds.
- Each extra tea adds 7 more seconds.
- Slip through a DVD to teleport! ฅ^•ﻌ•^ฅ
